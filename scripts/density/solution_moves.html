The web of small moves

Edge queens move more

With swaps only, a queen on the border can move in 1.3 to 4.4 times as many solutions as a queen inside, and about 1.5 times with moves of up to 3 queens. Once 4-queen moves are allowed, almost every queen can move and the difference nearly goes away.

The web joins up at four queens

With moves of up to 3 queens, the solutions split into thousands of small islands. With 4-queen moves, almost all of them join one group: 13,800 of 14,200 at n = 12. The joining moves are about half two swaps at once and half rotations of four queens.

What a move is

Two solutions are linked when they differ in exactly k rows. The queens in those rows trade columns. The search finds every such pair for k = 2, 3 and 4.
